Shaping Engineers Since 2000.

About the Department

The Department of Computer Engineering was established in the year 2000 with the aim of producing skilled engineers who can solve real problems using technology. The department offers modern laboratories, experienced faculty members, and a curriculum that is updated regularly with industry needs.

Students are encouraged to take part in coding competitions, research projects, and internships so that they gain practical exposure along with their academic learning.
