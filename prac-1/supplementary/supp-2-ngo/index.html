About Us

Our Mission: To make basic education, nutrition, and clean drinking water available to every child in the villages we work in, and to help families become self reliant instead of dependent on aid.

Aasha Foundation was registered in 2018 and currently works in 14 villages across Gujarat. We run our projects along with local village committees so that the community decides what it needs the most.

All our accounts are audited every year and the reports are shared publicly with our donors.

Ongoing Projects

Padho Aur Badho

Free evening study centres for children of classes 1 to 8 who are first generation learners.

Progress: 9 centres running, 420 children enrolled, 87 percent attendance in the last term.

Poshan Thali

A daily nutrition programme providing one balanced meal to children under six years of age and to expecting mothers.

Progress: 6 villages covered, around 1,100 meals served every day.

Jal Seva

Installation and maintenance of community water filters, along with training villagers to service them.

Progress: 11 filters installed, 3 more planned before March 2026.
