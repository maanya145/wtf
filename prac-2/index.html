Welcome to the Department of Computer Engineering

Building engineers of tomorrow through quality education and research.

About the Department

The Department of Computer Engineering was established in 1999. It offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in computer engineering.

The department has experienced faculty members who guide students in areas like artificial intelligence, web development, data science, and cyber security. Students are encouraged to participate in hackathons, research projects, and industry internships.

Academic Programs

B.Tech in Computer Engineering

A four year undergraduate program covering core computer science subjects along with practical lab work.

M.Tech in Computer Engineering

A two year postgraduate program with specializations in software engineering and data analytics.

Ph.D. Program

Research program for students interested in contributing new knowledge in computer engineering fields.

Certificate Courses

Short term courses in web development, Python programming, and cloud computing for working professionals.

Our Facilities

Smart Classrooms

All classrooms are equipped with projectors, smart boards, and high speed internet.

Computer Labs

Four dedicated labs with 150 systems running the latest software for coding and research.

Research Center

A dedicated center for AI and IoT research with support for student projects.
